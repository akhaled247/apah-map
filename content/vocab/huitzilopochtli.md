Huitzilopochtli (pronounced Weetz-ee-loh-POSHT-lee) is the supreme Aztec god of the sun, warfare, and military conquest. He served as the patron deity of the Mexica (Aztec) people, guiding them to found their capital, Tenochtitlan, and requiring human sacrifice to keep the sun moving across the sky.
