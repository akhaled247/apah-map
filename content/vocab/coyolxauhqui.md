Coyolxauhqui (pronounced coy-ol-shau-kee) is the Aztec (Mexica) goddess of the moon and the Milky Way. Her name translates to "Bells-Her-Cheeks" (or "She who is decorated with bells"), stemming from the golden or copper bells painted on her cheeks in traditional depictions
