Padmasana, or Lotus Pose, is a foundational, cross-legged seated yoga posture that translates to "Lotus Seat" from Sanskrit (padma = lotus; asana = seat or posture). It is designed for deep meditation, where each foot rests on the opposite thigh, simulating the petals of a lotus flower and representing spiritual purity, balance, and awakening.
