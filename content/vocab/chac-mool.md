A highly recognizable type of pre-Columbian Mesoamerican sculpture. It depicts a reclining human figure with its legs bent, head turned 90 degrees to the side, and elbows propped up. Typically, they feature a bowl or flat disk resting directly on their stomach or chest. They served as altars or platforms for ritual offerings.
