A native or inhabitant of the island of Niue, or someone of Niuean descent. Niue is a self-governing island country in free association with New Zealand.

While it is self-governing, it is part of the Realm of New Zealand, meaning its residents are New Zealand citizens and New Zealand handles its defense and foreign affairs.
