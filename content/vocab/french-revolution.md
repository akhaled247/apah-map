The French Revolution (1789–1799) was a period of massive social and political upheaval in France that dismantled the absolute monarchy and feudal system. Driven by financial ruin, inequality, and Enlightenment ideals, it established a republic, ultimately paved the way for Napoleon Bonaparte, and birthed modern democratic values.
