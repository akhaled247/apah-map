Tenochtitlan roughly translates to "the place of the prickly pear cactus" or "among the prickly pears [growing among] rocks" in the Nahuatl language.The name connects directly to the Mexica (Aztec) creation legend and the location where they established their capital in 1325. . The site later became the foundation for modern-day Mexico City.
