Axis mundi is Latin for "axis of the world". In mythology and religion, it is the symbolic center of the universe—a cosmic pillar or bridge that connects the heavens, the earthly realm, and the underworld. The concept of the axis mundi is a universal archetype found in cultures across the globe and it represents a meeting place between the divine and the mortal.
