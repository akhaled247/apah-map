T’oqapu (also spelled tocapu or tokapu) refers to the small, square, geometric motifs found in the textiles, ceramics, and architecture of the Inca Empire. Functioning as a form of visual communication, each unique pattern represented specific social statuses, ethnic groups, locations, or accomplishments.
