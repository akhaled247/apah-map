In AP Art History (Unit 9), the term continental refers to one of the three primary geological landmass types that shaped the geography of Oceania (the Pacific). It describes large, landmass-based regions like Australia, which served as the original stepping stones for the earliest migrations

Islands of the Pacific are categorized by their geological formations: continental, volcanic, and atollian.
