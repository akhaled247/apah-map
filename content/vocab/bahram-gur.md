Refers to Bahram V, a famous Sasanian King of Kings who ruled Persia from 420 to 438 AD.The name translates literally to "Bahram the Onager". "Bahram" means "victorious", while "Gur" is a Persian word meaning onager (a type of swift, wild ass). The nickname stems from his legendary hunting prowess and his favorite game to hunt.
