In Persian mythology and epic poetry, a karg is a terrifying beast—often described as a large, fierce horned wolf or a combination of a wolf and a rhinoceros. It is most famous for being famously slain by the Persian king Bahram Gur in an epic battle from the 10th-century Shahnameh (Book of Kings).
