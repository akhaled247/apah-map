A method of graphically depicting three-dimensional objects and spatial relationships on a two-dimensional plane or on a plane that is shallower than the original (for example, in flat relief).

In linear perspective, the relative size, shape, and position of objects are determined by drawn or imagined lines converging at a point on the horizon.

Aerial perspective is the effect the atmosphere has on the appearance of an object as viewed from a distance.
