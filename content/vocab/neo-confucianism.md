Neo-Confucianism is a revitalized form of classical Confucianism that emerged in China during the Song Dynasty (960–1279 CE) and dominated East Asian philosophy for centuries. It combined traditional Confucian ethics with the metaphysical and spiritual elements of Buddhism and Daoism to explain the nature of the universe and human morality.
