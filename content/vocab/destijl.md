De Stijl (Dutch for "The Style") was an influential early 20th-century Dutch art movement founded in 1917 by artists Piet Mondrian and Theo van Doesburg. It sought to establish a new, utopian visual harmony by stripping away complex natural forms and colors in favor of strict geometric abstraction.
