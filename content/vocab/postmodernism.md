Postmodernism is a late 20th-century cultural, artistic, and philosophical movement that emerged as a skeptical reaction to modernism. It rejects the idea of absolute truths, universal progress, and overarching theories (or "grand narratives"), instead emphasizing that reality, meaning, and knowledge are socially constructed and relative.
