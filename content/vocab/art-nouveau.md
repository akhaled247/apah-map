Art Nouveau translates literally to "new art". Flourishing between 1890 and 1910, it was a deliberate, modern departure from strict, academic 19th-century styles. It is defined by organic, sinuous lines and nature-inspired motifs (like flowers and insects), seamlessly blending fine art with everyday, functional design.
