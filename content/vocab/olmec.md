"Olmec" literally means "rubber people" or "people of the rubber country" in Nahuatl, the language of the Aztec, derived from ōlli (rubber) and mēcatl (people). It refers to the earliest known major Mesoamerican civilization (c. 1200–400 BCE) that flourished in the Gulf Coast of Mexico, known for producing rubber and monumental stone heads.
