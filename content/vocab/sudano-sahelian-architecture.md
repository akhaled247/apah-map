Sudano-Sahelian architecture refers to a range of similar indigenous architectural styles common to the African peoples of the Sahel and Sudanian grassland. The traditional, rectangular or square box-house with a flat roof, made of pure dried clay, sun-dried mud, brick or cow-dung plaster.
