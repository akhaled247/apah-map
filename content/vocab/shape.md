Shape is 2D, having height and width, but no depth. It can be defined by line, color value or texture. It creates pattern and rhythm when repeated, can be organic or geometric, can be positive or negative, creates emphasis when varied in size, creates balance when varied in placement, can be objective or non-objective, can be distorted or extended.
