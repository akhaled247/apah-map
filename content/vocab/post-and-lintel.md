Post-and-lintel, or trabeated architecture, is a simple construction method using a header (lintel) as the horizontal member supported at its ends by two vertical columns or pillars (posts). Post-and-lintel is one of the four ancient structural methods of building; the others are corbel, arch-and-vault, and truss.
