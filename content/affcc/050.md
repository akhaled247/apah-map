# 50 — Vienna Genesis (Rebecca and Eliezer at the Well; Jacob Wrestling the Angel)

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Early Byzantine Europe. 
**Date:** Early sixth century CE
**Medium:** Illuminated manuscript (tempera, gold, and silver on purple vellum)
**Location:** Syria or Constantinople (modern Turkey/Syria)

## Form

- Folio made of dyed vellum (calfskin)
- Gold and silver
- Size 12.5” x 9.25”
- Text at the top ⅔’s with visual along the bottom

## Function

- These are narratives from the book of Genesis from the old testament of the bible.
- Luxury item

## Content

- Story from Genesis 24, Abraham finding a wife for his son Isaac
- Jacob wrestling the Angel - Genesis 32

## Context

- One of the oldest surviving illustrated biblical books
- Named for its current location
- Medieval texts were for the wealthy of society
  - Labor intensive
  - Used expensive materials
- Created in transition of style from realism to abstraction
- Books from this period became increasingly decorative as a result of the new design (bound pages) and durable materials such as vellum and parchment.
- Books produced prior to the invention of the printing press are usually referred to as illuminated manuscripts.
  - Illumination means to brighten or adorn
