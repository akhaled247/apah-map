# 92 — Woman Holding a Balance

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Johannes Vermeer. 
**Date:** c. 1664 CE
**Medium:** Oil on canvas
**Location:** Delft, Netherlands

## Form

- Window on left, curtains drawn, yet still allowing some light to reflect upon the wall.
- Painting behind woman provides contrast to her head covering
- Predominantly dark painting, strong shadow from upper left, arcing to lower right. Then balanced through painting in upper right
- Smooth and soft application of paint
- Dimensions: 15 ⅝” x 14”

## Function

- Could be a genre scene or an allegory (both?)
  - Allegory: a story, poem, or picture that can be interpreted to reveal a hidden meaning, typically a moral or political one.
- Showing a time of weighing or judgment

## Content

- Woman in fine dress, appears to be from a wealthy merchant family
- Fur lined coat, light linen head covering
- Holding an empty balance in hand
- On the table are open jewelry boxes with necklaces and coins, as well as dark blue fabric
- Painting of the last judgment on the wall behind the woman.

## Context

- Dutch Baroque period
- Vermeer and family were Catholic, yet in Protestant country
