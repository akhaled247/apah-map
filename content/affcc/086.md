# 86 — Henri IV Receives the Portrait of Marie de' Medici, from the Marie de' Medici Cycle

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Peter Paul Rubens. 
**Date:** 1621–1625 CE
**Medium:** Oil on canvas
**Location:** Antwerp, Belgium (painted for Paris, France)

## Form

- Dimensions: approximately 13’ x 9.5’
- 24 paintings in the series
- Image was Flemish Baroque

## Function

- Intended for the interior of the Luxembourg Palace

## Content

- Scenes from the life of Marie de’ Medici, with this specific image depicting idealized marriage negotiations between France and Florence. There were financial and political benefits for Henri with this arrangement.
- Includes Jupiter (eagle) and Juno (peacock), Hymen and Cupid,  personification of France, indicating this was a marriage ordained by the gods.

## Context

- Marie de’ Medici of the Italian Medici’s
- Marriages were often to create political alliances
- Rubens
  - Baroque period
- had large workshop with plenty of assistants executing certain parts of the paintings
