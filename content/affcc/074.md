# 74 — Adam and Eve

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Albrecht Dürer. 
**Date:** 1504 CE
**Medium:** Engraving
**Location:** Nuremberg, Germany

## Form

- Hatching and cross hatching that define the planes of the forms
- Classical Contrapposto
- [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZdICgvMaH8A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZdICgvMaH8A)

## Function

- Creating works that could be reproduced allows greater opportunity for making money
- For collectors, not necessarily for devotional purposes
- Shows awareness of classical traditions

## Content

- Adam and Eve
- Dark forest that is distinctively German
- Bird, Serpent, Mountain goat, Rat, Cat, Bull (ox?), Rabbit, elk
- Sign with Durer’s name in latin

## Context

- Durer
  - Born in Nuremberg, one of the strongest artistic and commercial centers in Europe during the fifteenth and sixteenth centuries.
  - brilliant painter, draftsman, and writer, though his first and probably greatest artistic impact was in the medium of printmaking.
  - More than any other northern European artist, Dürer was engaged by the artistic practices and theoretical interests of Italy.
  - AD visited Italy twice, observing works firsthand and developed an interest in the human form, writing 4 books on human proportions
