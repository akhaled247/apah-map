# 5 — Beaker with ibex motifs

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Susa (Ancient Near East / Elamite). 
**Date:** 4200–3500 BCE
**Medium:** Painted terra cotta
**Location:** Susa, Iran

## Form

- Ceramic - painted terracotta
- Decorative
- Repetition of images
- Monochromatic
- Stylized forms and geometric designs
- Divided into registers
- Approximately 11 ⅜” x 6 ½” in size

## Function

- Funerary item, similar items were found buried with the dead of the Susa necropolis.

## Content

- Ibex (goat)
- Hunting Dogs
- Birds, common to area
- Lines dividing spaces into registers

## Context

- Neolithic, from Susa, important city from Ancient Near East (modern day Iran)
- Susa was at the time an important regional metropolis
- Was the location where the Code of Hammurabi was found
- The ceramics predated the COH
