# 78 — Entombment of Christ

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Jacopo da Pontormo. 
**Date:** 1525–1528 CE
**Medium:** Oil on wood
**Location:** Santa Felicita, Florence, Italy

## Form

- Arched picture plane
- O - shaped composition
- Light blues and pinks contrast a dark ground
- Color placement and implied lines encourage the viewer’s eye to move continually through the work.
- Almost chaotic use of implied line contributes to emotion of the moment
- Oil on wood

## Function

- Part of the decorative cycle of the Santa Felicitia Chapel in Florence

## Content

- Absent from the work is both the cross and the tomb, so it is unclear if is a deposition or entombment
- Christ, Mary and mourners
- Void created by O composition, contributes to the idea of loss

## Context

- Rejects many of the ideas of the Renaissance
  - Perspective, illusionary space
  - Naturalism of the body proportions
  - Stability of the composition
  - Protestant reformation began nearly a decade earlier
