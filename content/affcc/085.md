# 85 — Calling of Saint Matthew

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Caravaggio. 
**Date:** c. 1597–1601 CE
**Medium:** Oil on canvas
**Location:** San Luigi dei Francesi, Rome, Italy

## Form

- Oil on canvas
  - Dimension: 127 in × 130 in, near square format
  - Low key, use of tenebrism
  - Dingy, tavern setting
  - Light directs diagonals
  - Light shape upper right balances grouping of images in the lower and middle left

## Function

- ContraellaChapel paintings

## Content

- Clothing of the day

## Context

- Caravaggio made it a practice to paint individuals directly from life. This was a departure from the Renaissance idealized form.
- Caravaggio’s approach to painting naturalism was sometimes at odds with the function of the altarpieces as the focus of devotional practice.
