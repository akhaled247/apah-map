# 76 — School of Athens

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Raphael. 
**Date:** 1509–1511 CE
**Medium:** Fresco
**Location:** Stanza della Segnatura, Vatican Palace, Vatican City

## Form

- Plato and Aristotle grouped in center of composition surrounded by others
  - Holding books they’ve authored
  - Plato - looking on theoretical, unseen vs Aristotle’s focus on observable
  - Represented by gestures and colors
    - Purple - ethereal
    - Red - fire
    - Brown - earth
    - Blue - water
  - This division is continued by the groups on each side of the composition
- Strong linear perspective
- Varied division and symmetry with the groups allows open steps at the front for decent of Plato and Aristotle
- Natural poses, movements, interactions

## Function

- Room was originally a library, place of study

## Content

- Four branches of knowledge represented
  - Philosophy/sciences
    - Represented by The School of Athens
      - Those represented were not all contemporaries
      - Philosophers
      - Mathematicians
        - Pythagoras: harmony in music/math
        - Euclid: geometry, practical
        - Roman architecture has coffered barrel vaults, pilasters
        - Classical Sculpture
          - Apollo (sun, music, poetry)
          - Athena (war, wisdom)
      - Sharing of knowledge, change from medieval period
      - Included a self portrait looking outward from right
      - Full of pagan thinkers, not Christians
  - Theology
  - Poetry
  - Justice

## Context

- During the High Renaissance
- Same time and very close to Michelangelo working on the Sistine Chapel
- Pope Julius II
- Humanist/Classical united with teachings of church
- Not a large space
- Part of the Papal apartment
- Euclid was based on Bramante
  - Friend of Raphael
  - Architect, asked by Pope Julius II to create a model for a new Saint Peters.
  - Design based on circles and squares, evident in design of architecture in the School of Athens
- Homage to Michelangelo (portrait)
- Artists have moved from being craftsmen to intellectuals
