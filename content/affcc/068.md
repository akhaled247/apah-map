# 68 — The Arnolfini Portrait

## Attribution

**Culture / Artist:** Jan van Eyck. 
**Date:** 1434 CE
**Medium:** Oil on wood
**Location:** Bruges, Belgium

## Form

- Northern Renaissance/Early Netherlandish
- Near symmetrical composition
- Colors used symbolically
  - Green, black and red colors were costly and difficult to dye and therefore conveyed an idea of wealth
  - Blue undergarment referenced Christianity and the virgin Mary, but not in a overt fashion
- Mirror with painted roundels referenced Christianity
- Jan van Eyck was here painted above the mirror

## Function

- Possible memorial piece to the bride (posthumously painted)
- Possible marriage portrait
- Possibly illustrates the woman being subordinate to the husband

## Content

- Mirror
- Gargoyles
- Bed
- Chandelier
- Dog
- windows/glass
- Oranges
- Expensive fabrics
- Artist’s signature
- Shoes
